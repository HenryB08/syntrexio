## HOW TO USE THIS (internal, delete before sending)

**When this goes out.** Within 24 hours of the call that ends the diagnostic, or within 24 hours of the discovery call if the next step is the diagnostic itself. Proposals sent inside a day close at meaningfully higher rates than those sent after three days. There is no version of this that is worth being late for.

**What this is not.** This is not a free scoping document. Never send a proposal containing scoped work to someone who has not paid for the AI Systems Diagnostic. The diagnostic is the proposal for a first engagement. This template is for the engagement that follows a diagnostic, or for a repeat or expansion engagement with an existing client.

**Three options, never one price.** Tiered proposals win more often than single-price proposals, and bundled pricing is approved more often than line-item pricing. Never send one number. Never send four.

**Open with the answer.** Section 1 is what they should do. Not who we are, not our process, not a company bio. If the reader stops after page one they should already know the recommendation and the price.

**Review it live.** Do not email this and wait. Walk them through it on a call, with as many stakeholders in the room as you can get. Ask for the decision at the end of that call.

**Customize every time. Non-negotiable:**

| Must be rewritten for every proposal | Why |
|---|---|
| Section 1, the recommendation | It is the whole proposal |
| Section 2, their situation in their words | Quote them. Paraphrase reads as a template |
| The guaranteed metric, baseline, and window | One metric. Measured. Named in writing |
| Which of the three options is marked Recommended | Usually the middle one. Never all three |
| Week one deliverable in Section 6 | It has to be a real, working thing |
| Timeline dates in Section 5 | Real dates, not "week 1, week 2" |

**Never do:** invent a price, invent a statistic, name a client who is not HALT Fire, Doughbrik's Wavers, or Kinetix, claim headcount, guarantee revenue or closed deals, or offer a payment plan as a concession after a price objection. Payment plans are in the proposal from the start or they are not offered.

**Pricing.** Pull every number from the pricing reference at the bottom of this file. If the service is not listed there, price it at projected human hours times $350 per hour, then check it against market rate. Do not invent numbers.

## 8. What happens in week one

The moment this is signed:

**Hour 0 to 24**
- Written kickoff summary in your inbox: scope, success metrics with numbers, what is in and out, how changes get handled, response time expectations, and your named contact

- Intake completed. This is the one thing we need from you fast. Clients who finish intake inside 48 hours get better outcomes than clients who take ten days, consistently enough that we treat it as the single most important thing you do in this engagement

- Your business gets encoded: brand voice, visual system, products, pricing, policies, competitors, goals, constraints. Every output we produce from then on passes through it
