## HOW TO USE THIS (internal, delete before sending)

**What this is.** The written scope. It is the document both sides point at for the rest of the engagement when there is a question about what was agreed. It goes out within 24 hours of signature, alongside the access hub, the recorded walkthrough, and the 30/60/90 draft.

**Why 24 hours.** 43% of B2B churn happens in the first 90 days and 23% of churn is attributable to onboarding alone. The gap between signing and the first written artifact is the moment a new client is most likely to wonder whether they made a mistake. Close it fast.

**It must contain numbers.** "Improve visibility" is not a success metric. "Cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews for 12 of 20 agreed target queries, up from 2 today" is. If a line in Section 2 has no number in it, rewrite it.

**Everything in this document has an owner and a date.** If a line has neither, it is a wish, not a commitment.

**Ship it as a document, not an email body.** It gets referenced in month seven. Emails do not survive that.

**Customize every time:** all of Section 1, Section 2, Section 3, the named dates in Section 6, and the week one deliverable. Sections 4, 5, 7, and 8 are standing policy and change only when the policy changes.

## 4. How changes are handled

**Small things inside scope.** Ask. If it is under {{SMALL_CHANGE_THRESHOLD}} of effort and inside the tracks above, we do it and it does not become a conversation about money.

**New work outside scope.** You describe it, we price it against the published rate card within two business days, and it starts once you approve in writing. We do not begin unpriced work, and we do not send a surprise line item.

**Changes to the guaranteed metric.** The metric and its baseline are fixed for the measurement window. If your business changes enough that the metric stops being the right one, we agree a new metric and a new baseline in writing, and the window restarts. We will never quietly swap the metric to one that is easier to hit.

**Changes to scope that reduce it.** You can drop a track at the next billing cycle with the same 60 days notice that applies to the whole engagement.

**Everything in writing.** A change agreed on a call is not agreed until it is in the hub. This protects you more than it protects us.
