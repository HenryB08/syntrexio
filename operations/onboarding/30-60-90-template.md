## HOW TO USE THIS (internal, delete before sending)

**Mutual is the operative word.** The Foundation calls for mutual 30/60/90 day goals with owners and dates. Mutual means the client has lines on it too. A plan where every owner is Syntrex is a status report, and it teaches the client that their only job is to wait and evaluate. Roughly a third of the lines should be theirs.

**Every line has an owner and a date.** No exceptions. A goal without an owner belongs to nobody. A goal without a date is aspiration.

**Every milestone has one headline outcome.** If day 30 has nine goals of equal weight, day 30 has no goal. Pick the one that, if it happened and nothing else did, would still make the month a success. Bold it.

**Write outcomes, not activity.** "Publish 8 articles" is activity. "8 articles live and indexed, with 3 ranking on page 2 or better for their target term" is an outcome. Activity is what the failed vendor reported. Outcome is what the Receipt reports.

**Day 30 must contain something already proven.** The day 7 deliverable is already live by then, so day 30 is where the first measurable movement shows up, not where the first output does.

**Review it at every monthly call.** Mark each line hit, missed, or moved, and say which. A 30/60/90 nobody revisits is worse than not writing one, because it becomes evidence that our documents are decorative.

**Customize every line.** There is no generic version of this document. The examples in each section are shapes, not content.
