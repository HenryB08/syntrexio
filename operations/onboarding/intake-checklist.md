> Source of truth: SYNTREX_FOUNDATION.md v3.0, Section 13.

---

## HOW TO USE THIS (internal, delete before sending)

**Why 48 hours is the number.** A client who completes intake within 48 hours retains above 85%. One who drags past ten days retains below 50%. Onboarding speed predicts retention better than early results do. This checklist is therefore not administrative overhead. It is the single highest-leverage retention move available, and it should be treated with the seriousness of a deliverable.

**Send it at the same time as the kickoff summary,** inside the hub, with the walkthrough recording attached. Never as a follow-up the next day.

**Prune it before sending.** Delete every category not relevant to this engagement. A 90-line checklist sent to a Presence-only client reads as bureaucracy and slows the thing you are trying to speed up. A Visibility-only engagement does not need CRM credentials. Send only what is needed.

**Chase on a schedule, not a feeling.**

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| Hour 0 | Checklist in the hub, walkthrough recorded, kickoff summary sent |
| Hour 24 | Personal message to {{CLIENT_OWNER_NAME}} naming the specific items still open. Not "just checking in" |
| Hour 48 | Call, not a message. Offer to complete it live on a screen share, which usually takes 20 minutes and removes the blocker entirely |
| Hour 72 | Escalate to the economic buyer. Say plainly that the day 7 deliverable date is now at risk and what it depends on |
| Day 5 | Flag on the account health scorecard as an amber signal. See `operations/account-health-scorecard.md` |
| Day 10 | Red signal. Delays getting system access damage client confidence, and by this point the client has usually decided we are slow, even though the block is on their side. Address it directly rather than waiting |

**Offer to do it for them.** The fastest intake is a 20 minute screen share where we fill it in live while they talk. Offer this at hour 24, not at day 5. Half of all intake friction is that nobody wants to hunt for a password.

**Never let intake block week one.** Sequence the day 7 deliverable so it depends on the smallest possible set of items. Mark those items **BLOCKING** in the version you send, and get those five things first.

## C. The business itself

This is the part that matters most and the part clients rush. Everything Syntrex produces passes through your encoding, and the encoding is built from these answers. Two hours here saves twenty corrections later.

### C1. What you sell
- [ ] **BLOCKING** Complete product or service list

- [ ] Which offering makes the most money, and which one you most want to sell more of. These are frequently not the same one
- [ ] What you will not sell or will not take on, and why

### C2. Who buys
- [ ] Your best customer, described specifically. Not a persona document, an actual account

- [ ] Regulatory or compliance constraints on your language: {{COMPLIANCE_NOTES}}

### C4. Competitors

## F. The questions that must be answered before work begins

Do not skip these. Work does not start until every one has a real answer, and a real answer is not "we are not sure." If you are not sure, say so and we will decide it together on the kickoff call, but it gets decided.

1. **What does success look like in numbers, 90 days from now?** {{Q_SUCCESS_90}}
2. **What number would make you say this was worth it, 12 months from now?** {{Q_SUCCESS_12M}}
3. **What is the guaranteed metric and do you agree with it?** {{Q_GUARANTEED_METRIC}}
4. **What is its baseline today, and do you agree with how we measured it?** {{Q_BASELINE}}
5. **Which system is the single source of truth for that number?** {{Q_MEASUREMENT_SOURCE}}
6. **What have you already tried that did not work, and what do you think went wrong?** {{Q_PRIOR_FAILURE}}
7. **What is currently taking the most human hours in your week?** {{Q_TIME_SINK}}
8. **What breaks first if you get twice as many leads tomorrow?** {{Q_CAPACITY_CONSTRAINT}}
9. **Who inside your business is skeptical of this engagement, and what would change their mind?** {{Q_INTERNAL_SKEPTIC}}
10. **What is happening in the next 90 days that we need to plan around?** Launch, seasonality, audit, funding round, absence: {{Q_UPCOMING_EVENTS}}
11. **What would make you cancel?** {{Q_CANCELLATION_TRIGGER}}
12. **Is there anything you have not told us that would change what we recommended?** {{Q_UNDISCLOSED}}

Question 9 and question 11 are the two most useful ones on the page. Answer them honestly and they change how the whole engagement runs.
